Why 0Degree

India does not lack ambition.
It lacks safe first steps.

The system assumes linear progress: school, college, degree, job. When that path works, it works well. But when it breaks, there is no legitimate in-between.

People are forced into binary choices:
continue without clarity, or quit without support.

0Degree exists for that zero point — the moment when certainty collapses and direction is unclear.

We do not encourage dropping out.
We do not glorify disruption.

We support people who are already navigating uncertainty, and we provide structure, ecosystem, and small capital so decisions are made with evidence, not pressure.

0Degree is not about leaving the system.
It is about not being abandoned when the system fails to accommodate reality.

Because being stuck is invisible.

In India, if you are doing well, there is applause.
If you drop out, there is judgment.
But if you are unsure, confused, or between paths — there is silence.

No one builds for that moment.

0Degree exists for people at zero — not ahead, not behind, just uncertain.

We don't push anyone to leave college.
But if you already have, or if you are trying to decide responsibly, we stand with you.

Not with slogans.
With structure.

Because India has no safe pause.

Dropping out is risky.
Continuing blindly is also risky.

Yet the system offers no space to test, learn, and decide without permanent consequences.

0Degree creates that space.

We don't encourage exits.
We support responsible decisions.

Why "0Degree"?

Zero is not failure.
Zero is origin.

It is the point before direction.
Before labels.
Before pressure.

0Degree represents the moment where you stop pretending and start engaging with reality.

Not rebellion.
Not escape.
Just a grounded beginning.
