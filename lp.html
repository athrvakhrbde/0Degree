0Degree

Structured First Steps for India's High-Agency In-Between Generation

Pilot Cohort: 17 Fellows
Capital Raise: ₹1 Crore

India assumes linear progress.

School → College → Degree → Job

When this works, it works well. When it breaks, there is no institutional support.

Dropping out in India is high risk. Financial pressure, parental expectations, social stigma, no safety nets, and no structured re-entry make it a deeply consequential decision.

We do not encourage dropping out. But millions are already navigating uncertainty without support.

India has colleges, incubators, NGOs, and skill centers. It does not have a structured system for high-agency individuals who are between paths.

0Degree exists to provide structure, capital, ecosystem, and accountability. Not inspiration. Not rebellion. Not charity.

We are backing 17 fellows. Each must demonstrate proven self-initiated action, ownership under constraint, execution track record, and high agency.

We do not optimize for college brand, English fluency, resume polish, or social capital. We back movers in constrained environments.

The fellowship runs for 6 to 9 months. Fellows go through field immersion, structured experimentation, capital deployment, reflection with a public artifact, and a formal decision framework.

Each fellow builds one substantial real-world initiative. This is not theoretical exploration. It is execution-backed clarity.

Each fellow receives up to ₹5 Lakhs. Capital is deployed in tranches tied to milestones.

Use cases include building a small enterprise, running a structured pilot, research and implementation in local systems, community infrastructure experiments, or apprenticeship and field research hybrid models.

This is not stipend-heavy. This is action-heavy capital. We fund serious attempts.

17 fellows at ₹5 Lakhs each equals ₹85 Lakhs in direct deployment. Operational costs including program leadership, selection, partnerships, governance, documentation, and contingency are estimated at ₹15 Lakhs.

Total raise target is ₹1 Crore. Lean structure. High signal. No institutional bloat.

Capital is released in stages with milestone-based deployment, monthly accountability, and public documentation. Failure is acceptable. Irresponsibility is not. Capital discipline is central to the model.

Success is not unicorn outcomes. Success means responsible capital deployment, durable enterprises, informed return to education, long-term local operators, and an alumni network that compounds over time.

The outcome is long-term agency, not short-term optics.

India does not need more dropout mythology. It needs structured, responsible risk-taking in a high-pressure environment.

0Degree is raising ₹1 Crore to fund 17 high-agency individuals navigating uncertainty with seriousness. We are building infrastructure for responsible first steps.
