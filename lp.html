The Structural Problem

India assumes linear progress:

School → College → Degree → Job

When this works, it works well. When it breaks, there is no institutional support.

Dropping out in India is high-risk:

  • Financial pressure
  • Parental expectations
  • Social stigma
  • No safety nets
  • No structured re-entry

We do not encourage dropping out. But millions are already navigating uncertainty without support.

The Missing Institution

India has:

  • Colleges
  • Incubators
  • NGOs
  • Skill centers

It does not have:

  • A structured system for high-agency individuals between paths.

0Degree exists to provide:

Structure. Capital. Ecosystem. Accountability.

Not inspiration. Not rebellion. Not charity.
